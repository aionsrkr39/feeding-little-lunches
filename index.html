The lunch box grind

Packing lunch shouldn't feel like a second job every single morning.

Between picky eaters, allergy swaps, and the pressure to make it "look cute," lunch prep quietly becomes one of the most dreaded parts of parenting — and most of it comes back home untouched anyway.

The scroll trap

Pinterest-perfect bento boxes set an impossible bar, and copying them takes far more time and cleanup than any parent has before 7am.

The picky-eater wall

You pack something balanced, it comes home barely touched, and you're back to square one with zero new ideas for tomorrow.

The decision fatigue

Allergies, toddler-sized portions, and "no repeats" rules pile up until every lunch box decision feels harder than it should be.

The Sunday-night scramble

Without a formula to fall back on, planning a whole week of lunches turns into a last-minute grocery run and a stressed-out weekend.

Meet the book

A no-stress lunch formula from the founders of Feeding Littles.

Feeding Littles Lunches skips the fussy recipes entirely. Instead, it hands you simple, repeatable assembly formulas — protein, carb, fruit, veggie, and a fun extra — so you can build a balanced lunch in minutes using whatever's already in your fridge and pantry.
